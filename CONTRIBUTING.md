# Contributing

Kvist is not accepting external code, documentation, or other copyrightable
contributions at this stage. Pull requests from outside the project maintainer
may be closed without review.

Bug reports, reproducible failure cases, use-case feedback, interoperability
requirements, and design discussion are welcome through the repository issue
tracker. Please do not attach code or patches to an issue unless the maintainer
has explicitly requested them.

This temporary policy keeps copyright ownership and the AGPL/commercial
dual-licensing position unambiguous while the project is maintained personally
by Stefan Lindblad. No contributor license agreement is currently offered or
required because external contributions are not accepted.

Before accepting external contributions, the project will publish a deliberate
inbound-contribution policy. If commercial dual licensing is retained, that
policy is expected to include:

- an individual and corporate contributor license agreement;
- a perpetual copyright and patent license to the project steward;
- an explicit right to sublicense contributions under AGPL and separate
  commercial terms;
- contributor confirmation of ownership or employer authorization;
- identification of incorporated third-party material; and
- Developer Certificate of Origin sign-off as provenance evidence.

The future agreement should grant a license rather than require copyright
assignment. It will name the exact legal project steward in effect at that
time and will receive legal review before activation.
